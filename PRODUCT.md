# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Juan for senior full-stack roles, and fellow engineers who might collaborate, refer, or benchmark their own craft. They arrive with sharp pattern-matching — they've seen hundreds of portfolios and will dismiss a generic one in seconds. Engineers will inspect the source and notice the details. Recruiters are looking for signal that he's the real deal, not someone who filled a template.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Juan Camilo Cifuentes, a Vancouver-based full-stack developer with production experience across React, TypeScript, Go, and GCP. The site must communicate: he owns problems end-to-end, he has real scale behind him (400k+ users, $200M+ monthly volume), and he has the taste to build something this good. Success = someone closing the tab to send an email.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, technical, modern. Confident without being loud. The aesthetic should feel like the work of someone who sweats the details in code and in design — a developer with taste, not a designer cosplaying as a developer.

## References

Somewhere between Linear.app / Vercel and Basement Studio. From Linear/Vercel: the precision, the tight spacing, the dark surfaces that feel engineered. From Basement Studio: the stronger visual identity, the willingness to have a point of view, the sense that this was made by a person not a template.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap or any template-shaped portfolio — card grid, timeline, scrolling icons
- Dark neon / cyberpunk — aggressive neon on black, the crypto aesthetic
- Corporate / recruiter-bait — stiff, formal, reads like a digital resume
- Over-animated canvas hellscapes with floating particles and cursor trails

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** A developer's portfolio should feel engineered. Every interaction, every spacing decision, every transition earns its place — exactly as production code should.
2. **Identity over convention.** Avoid the default dev portfolio formula (hero + timeline + skill badges + contact form). Make deliberate choices that only Juan could make.
3. **Substance signals craft.** The content (real scale, real systems) is the strongest proof of work. The design amplifies it; it doesn't replace it.
4. **Precision as personality.** Restraint is a choice, not an absence. Tight type, intentional spacing, and subtle motion communicate more than decoration.
5. **The detail is the message.** Engineers visiting this site will notice what others miss. Invisible polish compounds into a felt impression of quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — reduce or remove transform-based animations, keep opacity transitions. Sufficient color contrast in both dark and light themes. Keyboard navigable.
